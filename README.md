# 🏦 Banking Customer Satisfaction Dashboard | Tableau

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Banking Customer Satisfaction Dashboard** is an interactive Tableau project developed to analyze customer satisfaction, complaints, response time, service channels, customer ratings, and banking service performance.

The dashboard transforms customer service data into meaningful visual insights that can help banking management understand customer experience and identify opportunities to improve service quality.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze banking customer feedback and service data using Tableau.

The dashboard helps answer important business questions such as:

- What is the overall customer satisfaction level?
- Which complaint types are most common?
- Which channels receive the most complaints?
- How quickly are customer complaints resolved?
- Which departments or services have lower customer ratings?
- Which areas have higher customer dissatisfaction?
- How does customer satisfaction change over time?
- What factors may be affecting customer experience?

# 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Customer Satisfaction

Customer ratings provide an overall view of how customers perceive the bank's services.

**Recommendation:**  
Regularly monitor customer ratings and identify services with consistently lower satisfaction.

### 2. Complaint Trends

Complaint analysis helps identify the most frequent customer problems.

**Recommendation:**  
Focus improvement efforts on the complaint categories with the highest volume.

### 3. Response Time

Longer complaint response times can negatively affect customer experience.

**Recommendation:**  
Improve complaint-handling processes and prioritize high-impact customer issues.

### 4. Channel Performance

Customer complaints and satisfaction can vary across different service channels.

**Recommendation:**  
Compare channel performance regularly and improve channels with higher complaint levels.

### 5. Service Improvement

Customer feedback provides valuable information for improving banking services.

**Recommendation:**  
Use customer feedback and ratings to identify service gaps and develop targeted improvement strategies.
